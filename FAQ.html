An executive coach is a large, comfortable vehicle for group travel. Typical features include:
  • Seating: Reclining seats with armrests and footrests
  • Safety: Safety equipment, first-aid kit, seatbelts
  • Amenities: Air-con, reading lights, tinted windows, curtains
  • Entertainment: Radio, CD/MP3, DVD and screens
  • Luggage: Overhead racks and under-floor storage
  • Accessibility: Kneeling air suspension for easier access
