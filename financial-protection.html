How our protection works

If your trip / tour is classed as a package holiday:

  • All customer funds are placed into a trust account in line with The Package Travel and Linked Travel Arrangements Regulations 2018.
  • An independent trustee oversees the account and releases funds only once your travel has been completed.
  • In the unlikely event of insolvency or bankruptcy, your money remains safeguarded and available for a refund.
  • We take protection seriously and meet legal requirements to provide a safe, reliable experience.

A quick overview of the trust account process.

What is a trust fund account?

It’s a separate account where your money is kept safely. A trustee will only release funds to us when your trip is complete.

How does this work?

  1. Your money is transferred to the trust account when you book.
  2. The trustee safeguards your money during the lead-up and travel.
  3. After your trip completes, the trustee releases funds.

What does this mean to me?

If a trip cannot go ahead and you don’t move to an alternative date, your money is returned. If the company became insolvent, the trustee refunds you — giving full financial protection.

Section 75 of the Consumer Credit Act

For purchases between £100 and £30,000, your credit card provider is jointly liable with the merchant for breach of contract or misrepresentation. If you have issues with goods or services and the retailer will not resolve them, you can seek a refund or compensation from your card provider. Section 75 ensures consumers are not left financially disadvantaged due to faulty products, poor service, or fraudulent transactions.
