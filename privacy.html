Why get one?

  • Excellent value if you plan to visit parks multiple times.

Things to check

  • Perks by tier (e.g. Silver / Gold / Platinum).
  • Activation rules, ID requirements and renewal terms.

The Merlin Annual Pass is ideal if you visit UK parks and attractions frequently. Choose a tier for the perks you value most, from fewer blackout dates to added discounts and event access.

Highlights
  • Multi-park UK coverage under one pass (tier-dependent).
  • Retail/food discounts, event access windows, friend tickets (varies).
  • Great fit with our Travel-Only trips — just book the coach.
