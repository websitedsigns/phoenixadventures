Disneyland Paris

The ultimate blend of Disney magic, thrills & unforgettable memories.

Overview

Disneyland Paris, located just outside the French capital, is the most visited theme park in Europe. It features two unique parks — Disneyland Park and Walt Disney Studios — plus Disney Village, hotels, and year-round entertainment.

From fairy-tale adventures to high-octane coasters, there’s something for every type of guest.

Quick Facts

  • 📍 Location: Marne-la-Vallée, France (35 min from Paris by train)
  • 🎢 Opened: 1992
  • 🎡 2 Parks: Disneyland Park & Walt Disney Studios Park
  • 👑 Icon: Sleeping Beauty Castle (Le Château de la Belle au Bois Dormant)
  • 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Perfect for: Families, couples, thrill seekers, Disney fans

Disneyland Park Highlights

Fantasyland

Step inside classic Disney tales: “It’s a Small World”, Peter Pan’s Flight, Dumbo, and the Fairytale Castle itself.

Adventureland

Explore exotic worlds: Pirates of the Caribbean, Indiana Jones and the Temple of Peril, and Adventure Isle.

Discoveryland

Futuristic thrills: Hyperspace Mountain, Buzz Lightyear Laser Blast, and Star Tours.

Frontierland

The Wild West comes alive: Big Thunder Mountain, Phantom Manor, and cowboy-themed dining.

Main Street, U.S.A.

Shops, snacks, and the best views of parades and fireworks down the central avenue.

Walt Disney Studios Park Highlights

Marvel Avengers Campus

Join Spider-Man, Iron Man, and more with rides like Spider-Man W.E.B. Adventure and Avengers Assemble: Flight Force.

Toy Story Playland

Family-friendly fun with RC Racer, Slinky Dog Zigzag Spin, and Toy Soldiers Parachute Drop.

Ratatouille: The Adventure

A 4D ride through Paris from Remy’s perspective — unique to Paris.

Hollywood Tower Hotel

The Twilight Zone Tower of Terror offers free-fall thrills and spooky storytelling.

Shows & Entertainment

  • 🎆 Nighttime Fireworks & Castle Projection Show
  • 🎭 Daily Parades featuring Disney characters
  • 🎤 Stage shows like “Mickey and the Magician”
  • 🎉 Seasonal events: Halloween Soirée, Disney Enchanted Christmas

Food & Dining

From character dining at Plaza Gardens to quick bites at Casey’s Corner, Disneyland Paris has options for every budget.

  • 🍔 Quick Service: Burgers, pizzas, sandwiches across both parks
  • 🍽️ Table Service: French, Italian, Tex-Mex, and buffets
  • 👑 Character Dining: Breakfast or dinner with Disney favourites

Planning Tips

  • 📅 Best visited over 2–3 days to cover both parks
  • 🚆 Easy train access via RER A from Paris
  • 🕖 Arrive early for shorter queues
  • 📱 Use the official app for live wait times & show schedules
  • 🏨 Staying at Disney Hotels gives Extra Magic Hours
