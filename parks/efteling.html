Efteling (The Netherlands)

Fairy tales, world-class theming, and attractions for every age — a magical European classic.

Overview

Efteling blends enchanting Fairy Tale storytelling with serious thrills. Wander the Fairytale Forest, float through dreamy dark rides, or brave headline coasters — it’s a brilliant fit for families and adrenaline seekers alike.

Quick Facts

  • 📍 Kaatsheuvel, Netherlands
  • 🎡 Fairy Tale Forest & immersive lands
  • 🎢 Big coasters + iconic dark rides
  • 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Ideal for all ages
  • 🎭 Shows, parades, and character moments
  • 🍽️ Plenty of family dining options

Headliners & Must-Dos

Symbolica
Dark Ride

Journey through the Palace of Fantasy with Pardoes — detailed sets, interactive moments, pure Efteling magic.

Baron 1898
Coaster

Story-rich dive coaster with a dramatic **37.5 m** plunge into the mine — superb theming and atmosphere.

De Vliegende Hollander
Water Coaster

The legend of the Flying Dutchman brought to life — atmospheric dark-ride sections meet splashdown thrills.

Joris en de Draak
Wood Coaster

Duelling wooden coaster — pick a side and race through medieval dragon lore.

Python
Coaster

Classic looping coaster — pacey, punchy, and a park icon.

Droomvlucht
Dark Ride

Float through dreamy forests and fairy realms — quintessential Efteling storytelling.

More to Explore

Thrill & Coasters
  • Vogel Rok — indoor coaster in the dark.
  • Max & Moritz — twin family coasters.
  • Piranha — river rapids adventure.
Classic Dark Rides
  • Fata Morgana — Arabian Nights boat ride.
  • Carnaval Festival — colourful world tour.
  • Sirocco — stormy spin with Sindbad.
Scenic & Family
  • Pagode — soaring views of the park.
  • Gondoletta — tranquil lake boats.
  • Stoomtrein & Stoomcarrousel — charming classics.
  • Halve Maen — swinging ship.
  • Monorail (snail) — gentle aerial tour.

Fairytale Forest

Stroll through beautifully crafted scenes from beloved stories — from Sleeping Beauty’s castle to Little Red Riding Hood’s cottage — perfect unhurried time together with photo stops galore.

Planning Tips

  • Arrive early to split time between coasters and dark rides.
  • Use the official app for live queues and show times.
  • Break up the day with scenic rides (Pagode, Gondoletta).
  • Families: prioritise Symbolica, Droomvlucht, Fairytale Forest.
