Parc Asterix

Parc Asterix is a popular theme park located in France that is inspired by the beloved French comic book series, Asterix. The park features a variety of themed rides and attractions, including roller coasters, water rides, and live shows that bring the world of Asterix and his friends to life. Visitors to Parc Asterix can also enjoy delicious French cuisine, souvenir shops, and unique entertainment experiences that make it a must-visit destination for families and fans of the comic book series.
