Overview

We accept many payment options for your convenience. You can choose to pay in full at the time of booking, allowing you to secure your purchase instantly.

If paying the full amount upfront is not feasible, we also offer monthly card payments. This option allows you to spread the cost over several months, making it easier on your budget.

Furthermore, we provide the option to pay weekly by bank transfer. This flexible payment method allows you to make smaller, regular payments, ensuring you can manage your finances effectively. Please get in contact if you want more info on payment options.

Our payment options

We offer multiple convenient payment options to make your experience hassle-free. You can choose to pay using either debit or credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer.

If you prefer using your debit or credit card, simply provide the necessary card details during checkout, and your payment will be securely processed.

Alternatively, you can opt for PayPal, a widely trusted online payment platform. By selecting this option, you will be redirected to the PayPal website to complete your transaction.

For those who prefer bank transfers, we also accept payments directly to our bank account. Upon selecting this option, you will receive our bank account details, and you can transfer the payment using your online banking service or by visiting your nearest bank branch.

Rest assured that all our payment methods are safe and secure, ensuring the confidentiality of your personal and financial information. Choose the payment option that suits you best, and enjoy a seamless shopping experience with us!
