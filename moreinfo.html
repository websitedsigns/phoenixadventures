Welcome to our coach trips website! We are a passionate team dedicated to providing exceptional travel experiences for all our customers. With years of expertise in the industry, we aim to make your journey comfortable, enjoyable, and memorable.

Experience the thrill

Discover our coach tours to theme parks and other destinations. Create unforgettable memories with Phoenix Adventures! With expertly planned itineraries, comfortable transport, knowledgeable staff, and outstanding customer service, we make every trip amazing. Whether you’re a thrill-seeker ready to conquer roller coasters or a family planning quality time together, our tours offer something for everyone.

Annual Passes

Thinking about an annual pass? With the right pass you can make huge savings and enjoy brilliant benefits across the year.

Why get one?
  • Excellent value if you plan to visit parks multiple times.
  • Perks such as in-park discounts, priority access windows and special events (varies by park/tier).
  • Some passes include free or discounted entry to partner parks *.
Things to check
  • Blackout dates and peak-day surcharges.
  • Perks and eligibility by pass tier (Silver/Gold/Platinum, etc.).
  • Activation rules, ID requirements and renewal terms.
Travel-Only option: We’re the only company offering Travel Only on our trips — so you won’t pay more than you need to. Use your own annual pass for park entry and just book the coach with us.

* Partner benefits and discounts vary by park and can change without notice. Always check the latest details with the park before purchasing.
