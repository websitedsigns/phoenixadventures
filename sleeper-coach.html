Why choose a sleeper coach?

Are you tired of long journeys in a normal, touring coach?

  • Surrounded by strangers?
  • Sat on a basic seat that doesn't recline fully so you can't get comfortable, and the person behind upset when you do recline?
  • No leg room or the ability to move around whist travelling?
  • Wasting time on the road or in basic hotels when you could be traveling to your destination?

Then look no further! Our sleeper coach service offers the perfect solution designed for your ultimate comfort whilst travelling.

Onboard amenities

Equipped with everything you need, a sleeper coach ensures a convenient and enjoyable travel experience.

  • A fully functional kitchen onboard — complete with a microwave, kettle, fridge, and even a coffee machine!
  • Cozy couches and chairs ensure there is a comfy seat to enjoy a chat and a nice cuppa whilst on the road.
  • Comfy, individual, private bunks provide a cozy haven for relaxation and rest.
  • Say goodbye to uncomfortable seats and hello to a good night's sleep as you travel.
  • Whilst there is a full sized WC there is no shower on board so we will make use of services/truck stops for the use of the showers.

Experience the convenience and comfort of a sleeper coach and make your journey a memorable one.
