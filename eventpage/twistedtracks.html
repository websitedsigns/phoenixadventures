Travel through Europe on a luxury sleeper coach and experience multiple theme parks in one trip. Ride headline coasters and enjoy an unforgettable journey filled with adrenaline, fun, and lasting memories!

Useful Info

The recommended age is 16+, but children aged 3+ are accepted when accompanied by an appropriate adult. Children must be able to sleep unaccompanied in a bunk.

Experience multiple parks over several days — from family favourites to Europe’s biggest roller coasters.

Travel in style on a VIP luxury sleeper coach with bunk beds, comfy couches, kitchen facilities, and entertainment.

Rest in private, comfortable bunks with soft bedding as you travel overnight between parks.

Tickets are not included — this allows flexibility for annual pass holders. Packages will be available ~8 weeks prior to travel if required.
