Useful Info

The recommended age is 16+, but children aged 3+ are accepted when accompanied by an appropriate adult. Children must be able to sleep unaccompanied in a bunk.

Experience multiple parks over several days — from family favourites to Europe’s biggest roller coasters.

Travel in style on a VIP luxury sleeper coach with bunk beds, comfy lounges, kitchen facilities and entertainment.

Rest in private, comfortable bunks with soft bedding as you travel overnight between parks.

Tickets are not included — perfect for Annual Pass holders. Packages may be offered ~8 weeks prior to travel if required.

Sleep in your own bunk, wake up at a new park, socialise in lounges and travel like a touring band on a nightliner coach.
